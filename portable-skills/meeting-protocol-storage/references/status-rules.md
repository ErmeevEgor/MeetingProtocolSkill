# Status rules for thematic blocks

Use these labels where they fit:

- `Согласовано` — an explicit agreement/confirmation was reached.
- `Информация зафиксирована` — the meeting established/described a fact but did not require a decision.
- `Решение не принято` — alternatives/proposals were discussed without acceptance.
- `Требует проверки` — a fact/action remains unresolved and needs confirmation/follow-up.

A proposal followed by “давайте так”, “всё верно”, “согласовано”, or equivalent may be treated as agreed.

Do not infer `Согласовано` merely because no one objected.
